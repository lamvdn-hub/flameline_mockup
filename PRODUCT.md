# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16, TypeScript, App Router, static export, plain CSS with design tokens. Chosen by the user 2026-09-27 so the demo can become the production base.

## Users

Primary: owners and buyers at high-end cigar retailers, lounges and corporate event buyers in the Nordics, evaluating whether to carry Flameline's portfolio and order through its wholesale portal. Secondary: enthusiasts and corporate hosts booking tasting evenings. All 18+.

Immediate user of this demo: the client (Flameline AB's owner), deciding whether to sign a website build package.

## Product Purpose

Flameline AB imports and distributes premium handcrafted cigars (Horacio and Barreda) in Sweden and the Nordics, hosts guided cigar evenings at the partner lounge Yxtobak in Borås, and educates on cigar culture. The site presents the house to trade partners, takes event inquiries, and gates a B2B portal with a shared price list and order-by-email.

## Positioning

One house holds the whole line: distribution, hosted events and education under one roof, with a resident expert (Ramin) and a lounge beneath a floor-to-ceiling humidor. Tagline: "The line from leaf to light."

## Operating Context

Borås, Sweden. Trade buyers evaluate on a laptop. Photos of brands, products, events and one portrait arrive from the client later. Swedish tobacco-marketing compliance and cookie wording are the client's responsibility. 18+ age gate required in production (excluded from the demo).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Demo scope: one landing page, hero near-final with a scroll-scrubbed continuous camera film (4 scenes, desktop only), body sections placeholder grade. Production scope (either package): 5 sections, real auth for 1–5 partner accounts, shared price list with 4%/8% volume rebates, order-to-email, technical SEO, Supabase Pro. Out of scope: online payment, per-partner pricing, partner-management admin.

Undecided: which package the client signs; final photography.

## Brand Commitments

Binding visual identity from the client-supplied v2 prototype: near-black ground #0c0a08, panel #14110e, hairline #3a312a, ember #d49152, amber #e7b46a, bronze #b89968, cream text #e9e2d3, muted #c9bfa8, dim #6f6350, oxblood #8b2d20. Cormorant Garamond (display), Inter Tight (body), JetBrains Mono (labels). Square corners, hairline rules, numbered section headers, fine grain overlay. Copy voice: measured, sensory, specific; no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

`Flameline Website v2.dc.html`: complete copy for all public sections, brand and series data, package pricing, catalog with SEK box prices. `flameline-ab-project-scope.md`: confirmed requirements and packages. No photography yet; do not fabricate client photos, testimonials or customers. Event prices and catalog prices in the prototype are the client's own figures.

## Product Principles

1. Unhurried over busy: one authored motion, quiet chrome.
2. Specificity sells: origins, vitolas, technique, never buzzwords.
3. Trade first: the B2B path is always one click away.
4. Truth in claims: only the client's figures and facts appear.
